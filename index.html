I completed my M.S. in Computer Engineering at NYU Tandon. My research interests lie in computer vision, visual perception, and generative models, with a broader interest in how humans and machines perceive, understand, and create visual content.

Since May 2025, I have been working with Prof. Jona Ballé on learned image compression, perceptual metrics, and visual perception. I also collaborate with Prof. Jiayuan Mao on intent grounding and structured reasoning for interactive photo editing.

Projects

Improving Face Perceptual Quality in Learned Image Compression

2026 · Ongoing

Research with Prof. Jona Ballé on face-aware perceptual objectives for learned image compression. We study Wasserstein Distortion with face-specific representations to improve facial perceptual quality while preserving texture quality at low bitrates. Manuscript in preparation for PCS 2027.

User-Guided Photo Editing with Multimodal Reasoning

2026 · Ongoing

With Prof. Jiayuan Mao, we study how vision-language models ground abstract user intent in parameterized editing operations through action search, visual feedback, and preference memory, helping users achieve professional-quality edits aligned with their creative intent.

Explainable Image Aesthetic Factor Ranking

2025

Applies machine learning to model human aesthetic perception from interpretable visual factors, using the AVA dataset to predict aesthetic ratings and rank perceptual cues that influence perceived image quality.

Real-Time Music-Driven Visual Modulation on Static Image

2025

Explores multimodal perception by mapping real-time music features to smooth visual modulations on static images, creating synchronized audio-visual effects that enhance human perceptual experience.

Accelerating the Design of Perovskite Solar Cells Using Active Learning

2023

Uses machine learning and active learning to accelerate perovskite material design through predictive modeling. Applies data-driven iteration to guide material design decisions.
